OnePlayer 0.13.3 has two mutually exclusive player dependency builds.

Normal OnePlayer build
----------------------
The normal unsigned IPA links MPVKit 1.0.0 and does not link KSPlayer/FFmpegKit.

MPVKit:
https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit
Pinned release: 1.0.0

KSPlayer Lab build
------------------
The experimental KSPlayer Lab IPA links KSPlayer 2.3.4 and its FFmpegKit dependencies.
It intentionally does not link MPVKit in the same application target because both stacks
ship overlapping FFmpeg and MoltenVK binary products and previously produced duplicate
link symbols when combined.

KSPlayer:
https://github.com/kingslay/KSPlayer
Pinned release: 2.3.4

KSPlayer's public repository is licensed under GPL-3.0. The KSPlayer Lab build is an
experimental test artifact only. The exact OnePlayer source snapshot is exported beside
the IPA and the upstream KSPlayer/FFmpegKit revisions are pinned by Swift Package Manager.
Third-party copyright and license notices must be retained in redistributed copies.

The OnePlayer media transport remains unchanged in both variants: STRM/Emby redirects are
resolved by the client and media bytes flow directly between the iOS device and the final
115/CDN endpoint. The NAS is not used as a media-byte proxy.
