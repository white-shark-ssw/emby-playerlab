Emby Player Lab uses the Streamyfin MPVKit AVFoundation fork 0.40.0-av.

Project:
https://github.com/streamyfin/MPVKit

Pinned release:
0.40.0-av

This fork includes vo_avfoundation, VideoToolbox, AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer,
FFmpeg, libass and other bundled components.

The published MPVKit-GPL product and podspec identify this binary distribution
as GPL-3.0. This app is currently intended for private, personal use through
TrollStore. If the IPA is distributed to other people, the distributor must
review and satisfy the applicable GPL source and notice obligations.

The previous official LGPL MPVKit 1.0.0 dependency was removed because its
prebuilt iOS Libmpv binary did not contain vo_avfoundation.
