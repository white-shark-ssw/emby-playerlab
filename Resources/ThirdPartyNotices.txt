Emby Player Lab 0.7.2 KTV cache experiment does not link MPVKit.

MPV source integration remains in the repository behind a compile-time availability guard,
but the MPVKit binary product is intentionally excluded because linking it together with
KSPlayer/FFmpegKit under CocoaPods -ObjC produced duplicate FFmpeg and MoltenVK symbols.
The active non-native playback dependency in this build is KSPlayer 2.3.4.
